Early harvest yields little: about 8-10 kg of hand-picked olives go into one litre, double-sorted, washed with ozonated water and pressed the same day at 20-22°C in our polyphenol-focused Oleapenol press. Single-grove oil is never blended; independent lab analysis, dark glass and nitrogen protection are part of the cost. At the table: a salad serving is ~15 ml, roughly ₺26 per serving.
