Balance in the Grove

Not to erase differences, but to bring them together in the right way.

Our story began with a dream of establishing an olive grove in Ayvalık.

Over time, we realised that a grove's character is not shaped by its soil, climate or a single olive variety alone. Different varieties growing in the same grove create a balance of their own through qualities that complement one another.

Sometimes the liveliness of one is completed by the softness of another. Sometimes one stands out through its aroma while another adds depth to the flavour. For us, good olive oil is not about erasing these differences; it is about bringing them together in the right way. This is where the idea of Olivamore was born.

We assess each harvest on its own, taste our oils and look again for the natural harmony that forms in the grove. The olive is a living product. Every year's rain, sun, wind and harvest time are different.

That is why making good olive oil cannot mean simply repeating a formula that worked in the past. It requires careful observation, tasting and the willingness to decide again when needed.

Our aim is not to turn every difference into a single taste. It is to preserve the character of each variety while creating oils that feel balanced, understandable and pleasurable at the table.

We carry the same approach into everything we make: ingredients whose origins we know, thoughtful production, simple contents and information we are ready to share. These are not preferences; they are the foundation of Olivamore.

To us, real quality gains meaning not through grand claims, but when a product's origin and the way it was made can be explained openly.

With every harvest, we search again for the balance shaped by the grove.

Our Mission

To respect the grove's natural balance and offer olive oils and olive products with a known origin, thoughtful production and a meaningful place at the table. 02 · Tomorrow

To become a Mediterranean brand that carries Türkiye's olive diversity and production culture to the world, known for its origin, flavour and reliability.
