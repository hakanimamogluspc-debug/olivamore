Why we started

This section is reserved for your real story: Ayvalık, family land, why you got into this. Not polished, but real. People buy stories, not products.

A sample flow: For years our family consumed our own oil; as we gifted it to guests, we kept hearing the same question: "where can I buy this?" Nobody knew where most of the oils on the market shelf came from. We did. Olivamore was born from the decision to write that knowledge on the bottle.

In the first year we filled a few hundred bottles from a single parcel. We're still small today, and we keep it that way as a feature: we taste every batch and write every label ourselves.

Mission

We promised three things

Transparency

Harvest date, acidity, polyphenols; we openly share even what the label doesn't say. We don't sell what we don't know.

Respect for the soil

No synthetic fertilizer, but cover crops. Good oil comes from living soil; we produce without exhausting it.

Real names

There is a producer behind every bottle and we say their name. Anonymous bulk oil is not our business.
