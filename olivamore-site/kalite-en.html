05 · Editorial standard

We separate information from product claims.

Olivamore guides draw on accessible sources such as current regulation, official institutions and peer-reviewed publications. General information about olive oil is clearly separated from claims that require analysis of the same product batch.

  • ●Each guide shows its publication or update date, sources and any material limitations.
  • ●Chemical or sensory values for a specific Olivamore batch are published only when supported by a matching report.
  • ●We do not infer unverified health, quality or origin conclusions; content is corrected when an error is identified.

A named content owner or expert reviewer is added only when the role and relevant competence can be verified.
