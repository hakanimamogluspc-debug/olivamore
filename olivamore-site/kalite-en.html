What we test, and why

Acidity

Free fatty acid ratio. Shows how quickly and carefully the olives were processed; lower means fresher.

Polyphenols

Natural antioxidants. The peppery feel in the throat comes from them; the most valuable marker of early harvest.

Peroxide

A measure of oxidation. Tells how much the oil has met light, heat and air; should be low.
